# Colantuono-Final-Project
This is my final for my Statistics 145 course where I analyzed players in the NBA Eastern Conference. I looked at the teams they play for, their primary position, even the college they went to before the NBA.

## **Instructions**
To access and utilize the script, open the "colantuono-final-project.R" file, which contains the code for my project. To access the dataset, open the "Sports Codebook - Data Table Tabular.csv" file.

## **Data**
The data collected for this analysis was all of the starters in the NBA Eastern Conference. The question being asked was how different really are the professional basketball players that start. Each unit of analysis is one player and there are 32 observations. The type of sampling that was used for this data was connivence sampling.

I gathered the data by finding all of the starters that are on each team in the NBA Eastern Conference. I then figured out their height, weight, team, age, college they went to, and more.
